# Render
Render is a platform-as-a-service (PaaS) provider that lets developers deploy and manage applications without worrying about the infrastructure.

Render = more modern approach for PaaS with a focus on 
- simplicity
- cost-effectiveness (better for startups)
- better performance for hosting apps, APIs, and websites

## Render takes care of 
- Hosting your app.
- Scaling resources automatically.
- Handling databases, files (static), and backend services.

# Agile
## Agile is a SDLC that some people treat as a religion 

## Agile is all about the following:
1. Customer satisfaction by early and continuous delievery of useful software
2. Welcoming changing requirements (even late in the development process)
3. Working software gets delievered quickly (in weeks instead of months)
4. There is cooperation between business people and developers
5. Projects are built around motivated individuals
6. Face-to-face conversation is the best way to communicate (co-location)
7. Sustainable development and aim to maintain a constant and low price
8. Working software is your measure of progress
9. Simplicity: Only build what is essential
10. User experience and good design
11. Self-organizing teams
12. Regular adaptation to changing circumstance 

## Parts of Agile 
### Def The sprint 
A sprint is a small period of time where you have an assigned task that you have to get the task done within that amount of time.

## OneDrive backs up your folders to make sure they are protected and avaliable across all devices 
### Can do Windows or macOS

When your files finish syncing to OneDrive, they're backed up and you can access them from anywhere in Documents, or Desktop.

## Your computer's system storage and OneDrive are different!!!!
System Storage (Local Storage): Location: The physical storage space on your computer's hard drive or SSD.

OneDrive (Cloud Storage): Location: Files are stored on Microsoft’s servers (cloud) and can be accessed via the internet

Key Differences between System Storage and OneDrive: (A) Storage Space: System storage is limited to your device's physical storage capacity. OneDrive offers storage based on your plan (e.g., 5GB for free or more with a subscription). (B) Backup and Access: Local files on system storage can be lost if your device fails unless you back them up. OneDrive automatically backs up files in the cloud, ensuring safety and accessibility.
